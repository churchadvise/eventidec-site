Where's That Verse?

Support for the daily Bible reference game from Eventide Apps. See a verse, find its home — guess the book, chapter, and verse.

Contact us

Questions, verse suggestions, or a problem we should know about? Email bibleappssupport@gmail.com — we read everything.

Restoring a purchase

All Access is tied to the Apple ID (or Google account) that bought it. On a new device, or after reinstalling, open the app, go to the All Access unlock screen, and tap Restore purchases. There is no charge for restoring.

I bought All Access but still see ads or a stop limit

Tap Restore purchases on the unlock screen so the app can find your purchase again. All Access removes the ads shown between sets and lifts the daily stop limit. If restore doesn't take, check that you are signed in with the same account that made the purchase, then write to us.

What All Access includes

One payment of $9.99 — not a subscription. It removes ads between sets, gives unlimited Journey and Library stops, and opens the full 60-day Daily archive. The Daily, the weekly Passage, the Sunday Finale, and the Bible reader are free for everyone, with or without All Access.

What are the support tips in Settings?

Entirely optional tips ($0.99 / $2.99 / $4.99) for players who want to support development. They unlock nothing and change nothing in the game — they are simply a way to say thanks.

How the Daily works

Every week the Daily walks through one passage of the Bible. Monday to Saturday you find that day's verse from the passage, piece by piece; on Sunday you play the passage's crown verse and put the whole passage in order in the Finale. Missed a day? The archive holds the last 7 days free, and 60 days with All Access.

What is the free stop limit?

Free play includes 20 Journey and Library stops a day; the limit resets each morning and only ever lands between sets, never in the middle of one. The Daily, the "Play your favorite" games, and reading in the Library are never limited.

Which Bible translation is used?

All verse text is from the Berean Standard Bible (BSB), released to the public domain in 2023. All 31,086 verses are in the app and work offline.

Accounts and privacy

There are no accounts and no sign-up. Your progress lives on your device. The game's privacy policy is at Where's That Verse? Privacy Policy.
